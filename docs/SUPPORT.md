# KeyPool support

KeyPool is operated by WEN LIU. Public installation does not issue an account.
Tool access requires a team token distributed by the operator and a KeyPool
OAuth connection with selected service permissions. No payment is required.

For general help and bug reports, open an issue at
[liuwen/keypool-plugins](https://github.com/liuwen/keypool-plugins/issues).
Include the client, plugin version, tool name, and a value-free error summary.
Never post tokens, private file links, confidential data, or personal data.

For account access, revocation, security reports, or data deletion requests,
email **nextcheckin@gmail.com**. Identify the affected account without sending
your team token or an OAuth credential. The operator may ask for information
needed to verify that you control the account.

Provider quota or subscription limits can restrict an otherwise authorized
operation. The skill reports those failures and preserves a provider you name.
KeyPool offers no guaranteed support response time or service availability.

The contact and these pages may be updated. Effective date: 30 September 2026.
